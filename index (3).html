Pet-care tips

Simple habits that prevent most common problems. Tap a topic to read more.

Feeding and water

Feed at the same times each day and measure portions instead of guessing. Keep fresh water out all day and wash the bowl daily. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions and xylitol, which are toxic to pets.

Exercise and play

Most adult dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of activity a day. Cats do well with two short play sessions using a wand toy. Bored pets chew and scratch, so rotate toys weekly.

Grooming at home

Brush short coats weekly and long coats daily to prevent mats. Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks and check ears for redness or odor while you are at it.

Vaccines and parasite control

Keep vaccinations up to date and use a vet-recommended flea, tick and worming plan year-round. Puppies and kittens need a series of shots, so ask us for a schedule.

Signs to call a vet

Call us the same day if your pet stops eating for over 24 hours, vomits repeatedly, struggles to breathe, limps, or hides and acts unlike themselves. Seek emergency care straight away for seizures, bloat or poisoning.
